## Subtitle

Build towers, defend the castle

## Description

Defend your castle from enemies advancing along a winding road. Build Archer,
Crossbow, Ice, or Bomb Towers on four strategic sites, upgrade them, choose
target priorities, and call down Arrow Rain at a critical moment.

Progress through five levels against normal, fast, armored, swarm, and boss
enemies. Earn stars and unlock the next defensive line.

Features:

- Four tower types with three upgrade levels
- Tower selling and a centralized gold economy
- Slow, area damage, and target-priority options
- Five levels, stars, and local on-device progress
- Portrait touch interface designed for mobile play
- Offline play with no account, ads, or in-app purchases
